1. Read the daily notes of the last 7 days, his open tasks, and the notes of the projects that appear in them.
2. Pick at most 5 items. For each item give one short reason: a deadline, a blocker, a promise made to someone, or work left half done.

4. Write the result as one note with `write_note`. The path is in the line "Note path" below. Start the note with a title line and the date.

Do not invent tasks. If you could not read something, say what you could not read.
